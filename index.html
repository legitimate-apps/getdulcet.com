Dulcet is a native music player for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV that plays the library you already host: Dulcet Server, Navidrome, or any OpenSubsonic-compatible server.

In development. Not on the App Store yet.
Follow along on GitHub Support

iPhone · iPad · Mac · Apple TV · Android phone and TV in progress

A real app on every screen

One shared core for the music logic, and a genuinely native interface for touch, trackpad and remote.

Your whole library

Browse artists, albums, playlists and favourites, and search everything, straight from your server.

Offline downloads

Download music to your iPhone, iPad or Mac and keep listening without a connection, in the original quality.

Synced lyrics

Lyrics that follow the song as it plays, when your server has them.

Quality you choose

Pick separate streaming quality for Wi-Fi and cellular. Downloads always keep the original file.

Private by design

No accounts with us, no analytics, no tracking. Dulcet talks only to the server you connect it to.

Open source

Apache-2.0 licensed. Read the code, file an issue, or follow the work on GitHub.

Works with the server you already run

Your plays are recorded on your server, so they show up wherever your server sends them.
