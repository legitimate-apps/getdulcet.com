Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026. Applies to the Dulcet apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Android, published by Legitimate LLC.

Dulcet collects nothing. We don't receive, store, sell or share any information about you or how you use the app.

What the app talks to

Dulcet connects only to the music server you enter, such as your own Dulcet Server or Navidrome. Everything it sends, including your sign-in, what you browse and play, your playlists and favourites, and your play history, goes to that server and nowhere else. That server is run by you or by whoever you got access from, and its own privacy practices apply to it.

What stays on your device

  • Your server address and sign-in, kept in the system's secure storage (the Keychain on Apple devices).
  • Music and artwork you download, plus a cache of your library so the app opens quickly. Downloads are kept in the app's private storage, are left out of device backups, and use file names that contain no server, user, artist or title names.
  • Your settings.

Removing your account from the app deletes all of this. Deleting the app deletes it too.

No analytics, no tracking, no ads

Dulcet contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party crash reporter. It doesn't track you across apps or websites.

If you've chosen to share diagnostics with Apple or Google in your device settings, crash reports may reach the app's developer through them under their terms.

Children

Dulcet doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

getdulcet.com uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date above.
