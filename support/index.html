Support

Dulcet is in development and not yet on the App Store.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub. Say which device and system version you use, which server you connect to (for example Navidrome and its version), and what happened.

Open an issue

You can also search existing issues to see whether someone has already reported it.

Please don't post your server address, username or password in an issue. Issues are public, and we never need them to help you.

Common questions

Which servers does Dulcet work with?
Dulcet Server, Navidrome, and other servers that speak the OpenSubsonic or Subsonic API. Navidrome is the server we test against most.

Do I need an account with Dulcet?
No. You sign in to your own music server. Dulcet has no accounts of its own.

Where do my plays go?
To your server. If your server forwards plays to a service such as Last.fm or ListenBrainz, it does that, not the app.

What does Dulcet collect?
Nothing. See the privacy policy.
